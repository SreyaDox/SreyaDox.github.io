On-Demand HPC and AI Platform (Oracle Architecture Center)
==========================================================

**Artifact:** `On-Demand HPC and AI Platforms on OCI
<https://docs.oracle.com/en/solutions/deploy-elastic-hpc-ai-research-platform/index.html>`_.
My name appears in the Acknowledgments.

The work
--------

An elastic HPC and AI research platform solution. The SMEs brought a
rough working diagram; I onboarded them to Oracle's diagram standards
and worked with them iteratively to convert it into the published Figma
architecture diagram. Some components had no approved iconography yet,
so I worked with Oracle's brand team to get new icons created.

Why it matters
--------------

Documentation standards only work if contributors adopt them. This piece
shows the unglamorous middle of that: meeting experts where they are
(a rough sketch), teaching the standard while producing the artifact,
and extending the standard itself — new icons through the brand team —
when the content outgrows it.
