Multicloud Private Connectivity (Oracle Architecture Center)
============================================================

**Artifact:** `Provision Private Connectivity from Oracle Integration to
Oracle AI Database@AWS
<https://docs.oracle.com/en/solutions/private-connectivity-oi-db-at-aws/index.html>`_.
My name appears in the Acknowledgments.

The work
--------

A multicloud connectivity solution spanning Oracle Integration and
Oracle AI Database@AWS. I created the architecture diagrams from scratch
in Figma, following Oracle's established multicloud diagram standards,
and developed them through consultation with the solution's subject
matter experts.

The downloadable draw.io version of the diagram — available from the
button below it — is generated by the :doc:`SVG-to-draw.io converter
<figma-drawio-converter>` I built in Figma Make.

Why it matters
--------------

Multicloud architectures cross product and vendor boundaries, and the
diagram is often the only place the whole design is visible at once.
Getting it right requires understanding the architecture well enough to
question the SMEs — not just transcribing what they hand over.
