The work
--------

A network topology solution for Oracle AI Database@AWS. Every diagram in
this piece is mine, created iteratively with the subject matter experts:
draft, review against the architecture, correct, repeat until the
diagrams said exactly what the design does.

Why it matters
--------------

Network topology diagrams fail quietly — a wrong boundary or a missing
path misleads the reader without looking wrong. A piece where every
diagram survived SME review is the cleanest evidence I can offer of
diagramming craft applied to infrastructure content.
