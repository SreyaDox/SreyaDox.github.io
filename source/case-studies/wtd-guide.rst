The Technical Writer's Edge (Write the Docs)
============================================

**Artifact:** `The Technical Writer's Edge: A Job Search Guide for the AI
Era <https://www.writethedocs.org/hiring-guide/job-search-ai-era/>`_,
published April 2026 on the Write the Docs community site.

The problem
-----------

Technical writers in the Write the Docs ``#career-advice`` Slack channel
were navigating an AI-disrupted job market with hard-won advice scattered
across hundreds of threads. The knowledge existed; nobody could find it
when they needed it.

My role
-------

I identified the gap, proposed the guide, and did the work end to end:
synthesized the community discussions into a structured narrative, wrote
and edited the guide, and shipped it through Write the Docs review.

Process and toolchain
---------------------

I built the guide in a docs-as-code workflow: Sphinx and
reStructuredText, version-controlled on GitHub, drafted and refined with
Claude Code. I structured the content to serve both human readers and AI
systems, so the guidance stays discoverable as more of the audience reads
through AI tools.

Why it matters
--------------

This is the project that best shows how I work without a mandate: find
the gap, synthesize scattered expertise, apply editorial judgment about
what makes the cut, and ship a public artifact a community actually uses.
